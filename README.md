## Overview

This repository contains a strategy deck I prepared as an independent analysis of the Phitku brand. The goal was to identify practical, low-effort improvements to the brand's website and to explore realistic directions for growing the product portfolio from a single alum-based format into a wider personal-care range.

**Deliverable:** [`Phitku_Strategy_Plan_By_Sonal.pdf`](./Phitku_Strategy_Plan_By_Sonal.pdf)

## About the Brand

Phitku is a D2C personal care brand that modernizes traditional alum (phitkari) mineral stone care into everyday hygiene products.

| Category | Current Products |
|---|---|
| Body Care | Deodorant bar, alum-infused crystal roll-on (Bulgarian Rose, Turmeric, Activated Charcoal, Aloe Vera, Natural) |
| Oral Care | Mouth paste |

## Problem Statement and Objectives

Phitku has a distinctive, ingredient-led positioning, but the online buying journey and the product range both leave room to grow. This analysis focuses on:

1. **Increasing conversion** on product pages by reducing buyer hesitation.
2. **Improving organic visibility** through structured data and better on-page content.
3. **Raising repeat purchase** through retention mechanics.
4. **Growing revenue per customer** through new formats, adjacent categories, and bundles.

---

## Part 1: Product Page Optimization

The idea: *turn the product page into a conversion engine* by making the buying experience clearer and building confidence at the point of purchase.

| Area | Recommendation | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Product Positioning & Pricing** | Communicate the value proposition clearly and explore an affordable entry-level variant. | Lowers the barrier for first-time buyers who are unsure about the product. |
| **Payment Visibility** | Show accepted payment methods near the purchase button. | Builds trust and reduces checkout friction. |
| **Delivery Information** | Add a PIN-code checker, estimated delivery timeline, and shipping details. | Answers the most common pre-purchase question and reduces drop-offs. |
| **Product Usage** | Replace lengthy "How to Use" text with simple, scannable steps. | Easier for new users to understand a product format they may not know. |
| **Product-Specific FAQs** | Write FAQs based on each product's formulation and real customer queries. | Handles objections on the page and adds search-friendly content. |
| **Customer Retention** | Introduce replenishment reminders, account incentives, and optional subscriptions. | Encourages repeat purchase for a product that is used up over time. |
| **Technical SEO** | Implement `Review`, `Product`, `VideoObject`, and shipping-related structured data (schema markup). | Makes the page eligible for rich results in search and improves click-through. |

## Part 2: Product Expansion Opportunities

Using **alum as the starting ingredient**, the strategy explores three expansion paths:

**1. Deodorant and body care**
- Deodorant spray
- Deodorant cream
- Different roll-on sizes
- Foot odour-control products

**2. Adjacent skincare**
- Aftershave care
- Underarm care formulations
- Facial care formulations

**3. Product combinations**
- Personal hygiene travel kit
- Body care bundles
- Deodorant + oral care combos

**Expected business impact**

- More value from the existing customer base
- More cross-selling opportunities across related products
- More reasons for customers to buy again
- Entry into new personal-care segments beyond deodorant

## Suggested KPIs

How the impact of these recommendations could be measured:

| Initiative | Metric to track |
|---|---|
| Product page changes | Product page conversion rate, add-to-cart rate, bounce rate |
| Delivery and payment info | Checkout abandonment rate, cart-to-order rate |
| FAQs and usage steps | Time on page, support queries per order |
| Technical SEO | Rich result impressions, organic CTR, organic sessions |
| Retention | Repeat purchase rate, subscription sign-ups, customer lifetime value |
| Product expansion | Average order value, cross-sell attach rate, revenue from new SKUs |

> These are proposed measurement points. No performance numbers are claimed in this analysis.

## Suggested Roadmap

| Phase | Focus | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Quick wins** | Low effort, immediate clarity | Payment icons, PIN-code checker, simplified usage steps |
| **Mid term** | Content and search | Product-specific FAQs, schema markup, review and video structured data |
| **Longer term** | Growth | Replenishment reminders, subscriptions, entry-level variant, bundles |
| **Exploratory** | New products | Spray, cream, aftershave, travel kit (subject to R&D and market testing) |
